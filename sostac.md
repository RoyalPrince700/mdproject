# SmartEdu Hub Digital Marketing Plan

## A concise SOSTAC presentation for management

Accessible Publishers Limited · September 2026

---

## 1. Situation — What is SmartEdu Hub?

SmartEdu Hub is an all-in-one education platform developed by Accessible Publishers Limited. It combines school management, teaching and learning, AI Books, exam preparation, competitions, parent communication, and finance tools.

It serves school proprietors, administrators, teachers, students, and parents from one connected platform. Its key advantage is integration: competitors often provide either school administration or digital learning, while SmartEdu Hub brings both together.

The platform already has early market proof, including adoption by more than 30 schools within three months and delivery of the Accessible Summer Competition.

### SWOT analysis

**Strengths**
- All-in-one SMS, LMS, AI Books, exam preparation, finance, and competitions.
- Accessible Publishers’ established education-sector reputation.
- Early adoption and real student-engagement proof.

**Weaknesses**
- Low search visibility and limited online awareness.
- Empty blog and news pages.
- Generic social links and limited audience-specific proof.
- Website messaging overemphasises school administration and under-represents learning, AI Books, exam preparation, and competitions.

**Opportunities**
- Own the “school management plus learning” category in Nigeria.
- Use SEO, content, Oxygen FM, NAPPS, school visits, and publisher relationships to build awareness and trust.
- Turn existing schools and competitions into case studies, testimonials, and referrals.

**Threats**
- uLesson has stronger awareness among parents and students.
- Edves and FlexiSAF have longer school-administration histories.
- Cheaper SMS-only tools may appear easier to buy.
- Competitors with larger content budgets may remain more visible.

### Where SmartEdu Hub is now

SmartEdu Hub has a strong product, credible education-sector backing, and early adoption. Its offer is broader than most competitors because it combines operations, learning, exams, and engagement. However, the market does not yet see the full value clearly.

The immediate marketing challenge is to increase visibility, explain the complete offer to each audience, provide stronger proof, and turn interest into demonstrations, pilots, subscriptions, and repeat use.

---

## 2. Objectives

- Increase SmartEdu Hub awareness among school owners, administrators, teachers, parents, and students.
- Increase qualified traffic to audience-specific website pages and improve ranking for relevant education and school-management searches.
- Generate more WhatsApp enquiries, school leads, and completed demo bookings.
- Convert qualified schools into guided pilots and paid subscriptions.
- Increase paid exam-preparation and competition registrations from parents and students.
- Create regular platform usage, school renewals, testimonials, and referrals.

---

## 3. Strategy

- **Reposition the brand:** Present SmartEdu Hub as a complete education ecosystem, not only a school-management application.
- **Segment the audience:** Use distinct messages and journeys for school decision-makers, teachers, parents, and students.
- **Build search authority:** Use useful, optimised content to close the SEO gap and capture people already searching for solutions.
- **Lead with proof:** Use demonstrations, school results, competition stories, testimonials, and Accessible Publishers’ credibility to reduce buyer risk.
- **Combine digital and direct sales:** Connect Meta, WhatsApp, radio, NAPPS, and school visits so awareness leads to a real sales conversation.
- **Design for retention:** Support onboarding and repeated use so pilots become subscriptions, renewals, testimonials, and referrals.

These strategies respond directly to the weaknesses and threats identified in the SWOT analysis and support the stated objectives.

---

## 4. Tactics

### Visibility and interest

- Rewrite the homepage to show school management, learning, AI Books, exam preparation, finance, and competitions.
- Create separate landing pages and short demo videos for schools, teachers, parents, and students.
- Publish SEO articles, news updates, case studies, and competition stories.
- Run audience-specific Meta campaigns.
- Use Oxygen FM, NAPPS, WhatsApp, and school visits for local reach and trust.

### Conversion and retention

- Use clear calls to action: **Book a Demo**, **Try Exam Practice**, **Register for a Competition**, and **Talk to Sales**.
- Follow up every qualified school lead quickly and move interested schools into guided pilots.
- Retarget people who watched videos, visited key pages, opened forms, or registered without completing the next step.
- Provide onboarding, tutorials, reminders, support, newsletters, and progress reports to encourage regular use and renewal.

---

## 5. Action

- Fix the website message, broken or generic social links, page speed, mobile experience, analytics, and conversion tracking.
- Create audience landing pages, audience-specific demonstrations, testimonials, and clear calls to action.
- Launch Meta awareness, lead-generation, conversion, and retargeting campaigns with separate creative for each priority audience.
- Optimise priority pages and publish consistent SEO content to rank on Google and Bing.
- Use WhatsApp, email, Oxygen FM, NAPPS meetings, and school visits to support lead nurturing and sales follow-up.
- Train the sales and customer-success teams to deliver demonstrations, onboard pilots, support users, and record lead status.
- Review campaign and platform data regularly, improve weak pages or adverts, and move budget to activities producing qualified conversions.

---

## 6. Control

### Performance measures

- **Awareness:** reach, impressions, video views, website visits, and search rankings.
- **Interest:** CTA clicks, WhatsApp enquiries, qualified leads, and demo bookings.
- **Conversion:** completed demonstrations, school pilots, paid school subscriptions, and paid learner registrations.
- **Efficiency:** cost per qualified lead, landing-page conversion rate, and return on advertising spend.

### Retention measures

- Track active schools, active teachers, completed learner sessions, and parent portal use.
- Monitor pilot-to-paid conversion, renewal rate, support issues, and reasons for inactivity.
- Use onboarding, reminders, training, customer support, newsletters, and retargeting to bring users back.
- Collect testimonials and referrals from satisfied schools, teachers, parents, and students.

---

## 7. Budget

- Meta ads — **30%**
- Content marketing — **20%**
- Website and landing pages — **15%**
- Search engine optimisation — **12%**
- Influencer marketing and user-generated content — **10%**
- Email marketing and CRM — **5%**
- Oxygen FM, events, and print — **5%**
- Google Ads test — **3%**

The percentage model allows the plan to scale to the budget approved by management. Meta leads paid media, while SEO, content, and website improvements build lasting visibility and conversion.
