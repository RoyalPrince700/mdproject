Content Marketing Fundamentals
Content marketing is the process of creating and distributing valuable content to attract, engage, and convert a target audience.
Common Formats: Blog posts • Videos • Social media • Guides & ebooks • Case studies • Infographics • Product/service pages

Goal: Attract → Educate → Build Trust → Convert → Retain

Search Intent & Content Types
Search intent = what the user wants to accomplish when they search.
• Informational - “How does SEO work?” → Guide / Blog
• Commercial - “Best SEO tools” → Comparison / List
• Transactional - “Buy books online” → Product / Service page
• Navigational - “SmartHub Edu website” → Brand / Homepage
Key principle: Create content that matches the user's intent.



Content Strategy
A content strategy defines what content to create, who it is for, and how it supports business goals.
Key elements:
• Target audience • Business goals • Topics • Content formats
• Keywords & search intent • Distribution channels • Publishing schedule • Performance measurement

Example: Goal → Generate SEO leads | Audience → Small business owners | Content → SEO guides, case studies, service pages

Content Briefs
A content brief gives a writer clear instructions before creating content.
A good brief includes:
• Primary keyword • Search intent • Target audience • Content angle
• Recommended title • Headings/topics • Competitor references
• Internal links • Call-to-action

Purpose: Improve content quality, consistency, and SEO alignment.

Topic Research
Topic research is finding subjects that are relevant to your audience and business.
Sources for topic ideas:
• Keyword research tools • Google Search suggestions • People Also Ask
• Competitor websites • Reddit/forums • Customer questions
• Sales/support teams • Industry trends

Good topics should be: Relevant + Useful + Searchable + Valuable to the business

Content Clusters
A content cluster is a group of related content pieces built around a central topic.
Example: SEO
Main topic: SEO
↳ Keyword Research ↳ Technical SEO ↳ On-Page SEO ↳ Link Building ↳ Local SEO ↳ SEO Tools

Benefit: Builds topical depth and creates opportunities for internal linking.

Pillar Pages
A pillar page is a comprehensive page covering a broad topic and linking to more detailed supporting content.
Example:
Pillar: Complete Guide to SEO
↓
Technical SEO • Keyword Research • On-Page SEO • Link Building • Local SEO
Think of it as: Pillar page = Main topic | Cluster content = Supporting topics

Competitor Content Analysis
Competitor content analysis involves studying what competitors are publishing and how their content performs.
What to look at
• Topics they cover • Keywords they target • Content formats • Content depth
• Search intent • Backlinks • Internal linking • Content gaps

Goal: Find opportunities to create more useful, relevant, or comprehensive content.

Internal Linking Strategy
Internal links connect one page on your website to another page on the same website.
Example: “How SEO Works” → “SEO Services” → “Technical SEO Guide”
Best Practices:
• Link relevant pages together
• Use descriptive anchor text
• Link important pages where relevant
Help users and search engines discover related content


Backlinks & Link Building
A backlink is a link from another website to your website.
Link building is the process of earning or acquiring relevant backlinks.
Common Methods:
• Link-worthy content • Digital PR • Guest contributions
• Industry directories • Resource pages • Partnerships • Original research/data

Key principle: Focus on relevant, trustworthy links—not simply the largest number of links.

Local SEO Fundamentals
Local SEO helps businesses become more visible when people search for products or services in a specific location.
Important areas:
• Google Business Profile • Consistent business information • Local keywords
• Reviews • Local citations/directories • Location/service pages • Local backlinks

Example: Instead of “book publishers”, you could target “book publishers in Nigeria”.

Summary
SEO Content Process:
Research → Strategy → Topic Selection → Content Brief → Create Content → Internal Linking → Promotion/Link Building → Measure → Improve
Key takeaway: The goal isn't simply to publish more content.

Create the right content for the right audience, search intent, and business goal.
