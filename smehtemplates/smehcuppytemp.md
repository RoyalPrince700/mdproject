1.Executive Summary
SmartEdu Hub is Nigeria’s all-in-one AI-powered school management and digital learning platform. Beyond administration and classroom tools, it runs national academic competitions that inspire excellence and reward outstanding learners with cash prizes, devices, educational gifts, vacation trips, and national rankings.
Accessible Publishers Limited proposes that the Cuppy Foundation become a named sponsor of SmartEdu Hub competitions — creating a high-visibility education initiative aligned with youth empowerment, inclusive education, and lifelong learning. Sponsorship is reinforced by AI Books and the Accessible Publishers educational book catalogue already available on the platform.
2.About Florence Otedola and the Cuppy Foundation
Florence Ifeoluwa Otedola (DJ Cuppy) founded the Cuppy Foundation in 2018 to empower youth through education under SDG 4. The Foundation supports girls’ education, child protection, and pathways for persons with disabilities, partners with Save the Children, and has launched hardship funds for African students at Oxford, NYU, and King’s College London. Cuppy is also the first international ambassador for The King’s Trust.
3.About Accessible Publishers Limited
Accessible Publishers Limited is an indigenous Nigerian education company headquartered in Ibadan, established in 1996, with national reach across 36 states and about 250 staff. SmartEdu Hub is developed internally. In 2025 the company was recognised as Nigeria’s Most Innovative Educational Publishing Company of the Year.
The company is ISO 9001 certified. Quality assurance is built into editorial review, curriculum alignment, print production, accessible formats, and SmartEdu Hub development, testing, and support. Documented quality management gives the Cuppy Foundation a partner whose processes, not only its promises, are independently standardised.

3a.ISO Certification and Quality Assurance
ISO 9001 certification confirms that Accessible Publishers Limited plans, documents, reviews, and improves its work against an internationally recognised quality management standard. Editorial and curriculum QA covers accuracy and school-readiness of titles and digital content. Production and digital assurance covers print quality, platform testing, competition scoring, learner data controls, and support. For this partnership, ISO-backed processes mean competition operations, learning content, and impact reporting are run with traceable ownership and continuous improvement after each season.
