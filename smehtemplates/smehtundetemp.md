1.Executive Summary
SmartEdu Hub is Nigeria’s all-in-one AI-powered school management and digital learning platform. It combines school operations, assessment, AI Books, Accessible Publishers educational titles, and national academic competitions that reward excellence with cash prizes, devices, educational gifts, vacation trips, and rankings.
Accessible Publishers Limited proposes a partnership with Tunde Onakoya and Chess in Slums Africa in which SmartEdu Hub competitions and learning resources extend CISA’s competitive, mentorship-driven model into broader academic achievement — while AI Books and Accessible Publishers titles reinforce literacy and curriculum mastery for champions in underserved communities.
2.About Tunde Onakoya and Chess in Slums Africa
Tunde Onakoya founded Chess in Slums Africa in 2018 to empower children in underserved communities through chess, education, technology, and mentorship. CISA has grown from community chess interventions into scholarships, STEM and digital skills, socio-emotional development, and an Innovation Hub in Lagos offering robotics, coding, AI exposure, design, and civic education. Onakoya holds the Guinness World Record for the longest chess marathon and continues to advocate for quality education for African children.
3.About Accessible Publishers Limited
Accessible Publishers Limited is an indigenous Nigerian education company headquartered in Ibadan, established in 1996, with national reach across 36 states and about 250 staff. SmartEdu Hub is developed internally. In 2025 the company was recognised as Nigeria’s Most Innovative Educational Publishing Company of the Year.
The company is ISO 9001 certified. Quality assurance is built into editorial review, curriculum alignment, print production, accessible formats, and SmartEdu Hub development, testing, and support. Documented quality management gives Chess in Slums Africa a partner whose processes, not only its promises, are independently standardised.

3a.ISO Certification and Quality Assurance
ISO 9001 certification confirms that Accessible Publishers Limited plans, documents, reviews, and improves its work against an internationally recognised quality management standard. Editorial and curriculum QA covers accuracy and school-readiness of titles and digital content. Production and digital assurance covers print quality, platform testing, competition scoring, learner data controls, and support. For this partnership, ISO-backed processes mean competition operations, book access, hub onboarding, and outcome reporting are run with traceable ownership and continuous improvement after each pilot.
