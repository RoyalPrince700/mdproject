4.Overview of SmartEdu Hub
SmartEdu Hub is an integrated platform that combines school management and learning management features. It supports both the academic and administrative sides of school operations, enabling schools and education partners to reduce manual processes, improve accountability, provide learners with digital resources, and support teachers with AI-enabled tools and curriculum-based content.
SmartEdu Hub Component	Description
School Management System	Attendance, academic records, attribute grading, parent/teacher engagement, and learner tracking.
Administrative and Financial Support platform	Teacher/learner profiling, school fee records, administrative reports, financial reports, and AiMessage to teachers and parents.
Computer-Based Tests and Past Questions	CBT for all classes, special CBT for terminal classes, past questions including WAEC, UTME, NECO, BECE, and NABTEB, with instant reports and feedback.
Report Card and Transcript	Customised report card generation, online report access, transcript generation, and online transcript access.
Video and Audio Lessons	NERDC-tailored video lessons, NERDC-tailored audio lessons, offline PDF lesson notes, and instant assessments on video lessons.
Students’ and Teachers’ iBook	NERDC-tailored textbooks, lesson plan and note generation, AI tutor for student preparation, common-mistake prompts, and flashcards.
