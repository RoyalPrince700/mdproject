4. PROPOSED SOLUTION OVERVIEW
Our proposal is structured around three core deliverables tailored to the basic needs of schools. Each component can be deployed independently, but the full power of the platform is realised when all three work together, sharing data, student profiles, and performance insights seamlessly.
Component	Description
iBooks (Interactive Books)	Curriculum-aligned, multimedia-rich digital textbooks designed for use on tablets, laptops, and smartphones, with offline support.
The Computer Based Test Environment	A comprehensive digital library of past question CBTs accessible to students and teachers.
School Management System (SMS)	An integrated platform for managing student records, attendance, staff payroll, examinations, parent communication, and real-time reporting.


4a. iBOOKS: INTERACTIVE DIGITAL TEXTBOOKS
Our iBooks are curriculum-aligned interactive textbooks designed in accordance with the Nigerian Educational Research and Development Council (NERDC) curriculum for basic education. They represent a transformative departure from static printed textbooks, offering an immersive and engaging learning experience.

Key Features
Lesson Plans and Notes: Teachers can leverage this platform to plan their lessons and develop a well-detailed lesson notes for teaching delivery in the classrooms. Also, they can download the document generated and present it to ministry supervisors. 
Question Bank: Every topic comes with different exam-bodies’ questions. Teachers can access or generate these questions to prepare their students for external examinations or use as continuous assessment in the classrooms. 
Suggested Videos: There are carefully selected educational videos attached to topics to help teachers explain concepts better and improve students’ understanding through visual learning.
Real Word Examples: Each topic is supported with practical and relatable examples that connect classroom learning to everyday life experiences, making lessons more engaging and easier to understand.
Common Mistakes: Teachers and students can identify common errors usually made in each topic area, helping learners avoid repetition of mistakes during classwork, assignments, and examinations.
Study Tricks: The platform provides simple learning tips, memory aids, and smart study techniques that help students understand faster, retain information longer, and improve academic performance.
4b. The Computer Based Test Environment
The CBT environment provides students with an extensive, searchable digital library of past examination questions from relevant national and state examining bodies. Questions are categorised by year, subject, topic, and difficulty level, enabling highly targeted revision and timed mock examinations.
Key Features
Comprehensive past-question database covering multiple subjects and year groups, continuously updated following each new examination sitting.
•Timed mock examinations that replicate the exact format and conditions of major external assessments, reducing examination anxiety and improving time management.
•Instant scoring and detailed answer explanations, that is, every incorrect answer is accompanied by a step-by-step worked solution, turning mistakes into learning moments.
•Teacher-set assignments: educators can create targeted practice sets for specific topics and assign them to entire classes or individual students.
•Performance analytics dashboard showing class averages, student rankings, topic-level mastery rates, and progress over time.
•Offline access: practice sets can be downloaded and completed without internet connectivity, with results syncing automatically upon reconnection.
4c. School Management System (SMS)
The Accessible School Management System is the administrative backbone of our platform. It is designed to eliminate paperwork, improve data accuracy, and give school leaders, SUBEB officials, and policymakers real-time insight into school performance across the state.
Core Modules
Module	Capabilities
Student Enrolment & Records	Digital registration and bio-data capture; academic history tracking from year to year; document uploads (birth certificate, immunisation records); automated class and section assignment; transfer management between schools within the network.
Attendance Management	Daily digital attendance for students and staff bio-data record options; automated parent app notification on unexplained absence; attendance trend analytics per class, teacher, and school.
Academic & Examinations	Automated and manual Continuous Assessment (CA) score; automated weighted result computation based on configurable grading schemes; rank and percentile calculation; digital report card generation and print-ready export; end-of-term result SMS to parents within the platform.
Staff & HR Management	Complete staff profile management including qualifications, deployment history, and performance appraisals; leave management with approval workflows; staff directory accessible to relevant administrators.
Finance & Fees Management	Student levy and fee collection tracking; income and expenditure recording; automated fee balance notifications to parents; configurable budget tracking for school administrators; audit-ready financial reporting.
Parent & Community Portal	Mobile app portal giving parents real-time access to their child's attendance record, academic results, upcoming events, and school announcements. Two-way messaging with teachers and school management.
Analytics & Reporting Dashboard	A state-wide or network-wide data aggregation layer that gives education officials live visibility into enrolment figures, attendance rates, examination performance, teacher deployment, and resource utilization, all filterable by school, zone, or local area.
Library Management	Integration with the iBook and eBook ecosystem; tracking of physical and digital library resources; reading statistics per student.

5.  Technical Specifications
Cloud-hosted on secure, data-sovereign servers with 99.9% uptime SLA and daily automated backups, with optional on-premise deployment for institutions with strict data residency requirements.
Mobile-first responsive design, optimised for Android and iOS smartphones and tablets, requiring no native app installation for core functions.
Role-based access control (RBAC): granular permission management ensures that each user, from a class teacher to a state director, sees precisely the data relevant to their role, and no more.
