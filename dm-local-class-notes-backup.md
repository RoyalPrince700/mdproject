Introduction to Digital Marketing
September 19, 2026

What is digital marketing? 
Marketing products or services using digital channels such as search engines, social media, websites, email, and online advertising.

Traditional vs digital marketing 
Traditional: TV, radio, print, billboards, etc.
Digital: Search, social media, email, websites, online ads, etc.
Key difference: Digital is more measurable, interactive, and targetable.

Major Digital Marketing Channels
SEO, PPC, social media, content marketing, email, display advertising, affiliate marketing, and CRO

SEO 
Improving a website so it ranks higher in search engines and attracts organic (unpaid) traffic.

Social media marketing
Using platforms like Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, and X to build awareness, engagement, and sales.

Content marketing 
Creating and distributing useful content—such as blogs, videos, guides, and social posts—to attract and engage an audience.

Email marketing 
Using email to communicate with prospects and customers, nurture relationships, promote offers, and drive conversions.

Display Advertising
Visual online ads, such as banners, images, and videos, shown across websites, apps, and digital platforms.

Affiliate marketing 
A performance-based model where affiliates promote a product or service and earn a commission for generating sales or leads.

Conversion Rate Optimisation (CRO) 
Improving a website or landing page to increase the percentage of visitors who take a desired action.
