# SmartEdu Hub — Product Brief

**Brand:** SmartEdu Hub (SMeH)  
**Parent company:** Accessible Publishers Limited (Ibadan, established 1996)  
**Category:** School Management System (SMS) + Learning Management System (LMS) + exam preparation + academic competitions

Use this file as the product source for digital marketing (DM) class activities. Do not treat SmartEdu Hub as “an app.” Treat it as a school operating and learning ecosystem.

---

## Positioning

SmartEdu Hub is Nigeria’s all-in-one AI-powered school management and digital learning platform. It helps schools simplify administration, improve teaching, raise student performance, and prepare learners for major examinations — from one secure environment.

Unlike traditional school software that stops at administration, SmartEdu Hub combines school operations, digital learning, artificial intelligence, communication, finance, assessments, and national academic competitions.

**Tagline options:** One Platform. Every School Need. · Manage Better. Teach Better. Learn Better.

## Product lines (who each line is for)

| Line | What it is | Primary audience |

---

## Core modules

1. **Explore** — school digital library: subjects, study materials, past questions, AI-powered books.  
2. **AI Books** — digital textbooks with an AI tutor (questions while reading, summaries, independent revision). Curriculum-aligned (NERDC).  
3. **Past questions / CBT** — organised exam materials from WAEC, NECO, JAMB, BECE, NCEE.  
4. **Dashboard** — live view of students, teachers, attendance, fees, outstanding payments, classes.  
5. **Communication** — private messages, broadcasts, role-based school messaging.  
6. **Teachers / Students / Classes** — workforce, enrolment, academic structure.  
7. **Assessments, attendance, timetable, results** — daily school operations.  
8. **Competitions** — cash prizes, laptops, educational gifts, vacation trips, national rankings.  
9. **Management and activity log** — sessions, promotions, grading; every action recorded.  
10. **Finance and subscription** — fees, expenses, scholarships, billing, plan access.

---

## Problems the product solves (plain language)

- Schools run on scattered paper, WhatsApp groups, and disconnected tools.  
- Teachers lack a single place for content, assessments, and feedback.  
- Students cannot easily find quality materials or organised past questions.  
- Parents cannot see attendance, results, and fees in one trusted place.  
- Bursars lose money and time tracking fees by hand.  
- Exam candidates are unprepared for WAEC, NECO, JAMB, BECE, and NCEE patterns.
