## About Ojamaker

Ojamaker is a modern eCommerce platform that enables anyone to create a professional online store with AI-powered features.

Built for modern entrepreneurs who want AI-powered selling, local payment support, and marketplace features without technical setup.

Ojamaker is ideal for:

- Entrepreneurs launching a new online business
- Small and medium-sized businesses moving online
- Creators and personal brands selling products
- Marketplace founders who want multi-vendor support
- African businesses that need local payment options and multi-currency support
- Businesses looking for a simple, no-code alternative to Shopify, WooCommerce, or Wix

Our platform includes:

- **AI Sales Agent**: An intelligent assistant that helps you sell 24/7
- **Beautiful Themes**: Professional, customizable store designs
- **Local Payments**: Seamless payment integration for multiple currencies
- **Multi-Vendor Support**: Build your own marketplace
- **Visual Search**: AI-powered product discovery
