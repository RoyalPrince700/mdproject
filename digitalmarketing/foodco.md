Situation Analysis
Background of the Organisation
FoodCo is one of the leading B2C multichannel retailers and the third largest supermarket chain brand in Nigeria. For over four decades, they have continued to serve families in South-west Nigeria with top quality products at affordable prices. 
FoodCo runs walk-in stores/malls and an online supermarket (click and brick) to help households in Nigeria get access to non-stop supplies of household items while providing an unparalleled customer service experience.
The organisation is segmented into four categories listed below.
FoodCo Supermarket
FoodCo is the second largest supermarket in South-West Nigeria, outside Lagos. They sell top quality products across a wide range of categories which includes groceries, household items, toiletries, apparel and so on.
FoodCo Fast Food
The brand operates both walk-in restaurants located right within FoodCo outlets and recently launched a food delivery web and mobile app where people can order food.

FoodCo Manufacturing
Aside from supermarkets and fast food, the company currently manufactures some products called “Sunfresh”. The Sunfresh products include eight Ice cream variants, five bread variants and bottled water.

FoodCo Arcade
To experience a premium all-in-one visit, FoodCo added arcade centers located within the FoodCo outlets.

Customer Analysis
Online & Offline Segmentation
Web Presence
FoodCo has a strong online presence both on search engines and social media judging by their number of followers. The company has about 13,140 followers on Instagram, Over 11,000 likes on Facebook and 3000+ followers on LinkedIn. According to the website audit done on Ubersuggest, foodco.ng has about 32,000 organic visits and a total monthly visit of 50.6k (according to Similarweb). The corporate website has about 500 organic monthly visits and 1,700 total monthly visits. According to Similarweb, FoodCo acquires users directly (people tying in their URLs directly) and from search engines. FoodCo has an active mobile app for Android users with over 500 download.

Geographic Segmentation
FoodCo only has footprints in the south-western part of Nigeria covering only three states (Lagos, Oyo and Ogun) out of the six south-western states, with an average of 6 stores walk-in stores per state (as seen on Google My Business).




Demographic segmentation
FoodCo caters for all genders, all types of income earners, religions, occupations, family structures and level of education. The customers’ age ranges between 13 and 65+ (Gen Z, Millennial, Gen X and Baby Boomer).
Customer Persona

Strategy
Brand Positioning
FoodCo has set itself as one of the leading retail companies in south-west Nigeria, helping its customers get all household items at affordable prices. FoodCo has been impressive in how they have positioned themselves in the retail industry. For the majority of consumers in South-west Nigeria, FoodCo is still synonymous with household items. As seen in the competitive market analysis, FoodCo holds the 2nd most visited retail website in south-west Nigeria, higher than two out of its three main competitors. Shoprite is the most popular because it is known and available all over the country. FoodCo’s positioning strategy will be to grow and maintain their “One store to each local government area” which has not been done by other retail stores while selling to their customers at wholesale prices.
Value proposition
“Marketing is not the art of finding clever ways to dispose of what you make. It is the art of creating genuine customer value.” – Philip Kotler
The unique selling point of FoodCo is selling to their customers at wholesale prices without having to buy in bulk at a discounted “wholesaler price”. While doing this, they also brought the store closer to each household by “establishing at least a physical store in each local government” within a state. By doing this, each state can have multiple stores depending on the number of local government areas in the state. 
FoodCo made itself unique by providing easy access to wholesale discounted prices without leaving your locality. The brand is also known for its customer loyalty program called “intervention” which allows customers to get a discount after a purchase higher than ₦60,000. 
Strategy using the RACE model
Reach
●	Create a strong Top of Mind Awareness (TOMA) for the brand.
We will be creating massive brand awareness for the brand to drive customers to the company’s online and physical stores by running targeted online marketing campaigns using paid, owned and earned media. We will launch an SEO-optimised ecommerce website to compete with competitors’ websites on search engines and target in-market audiences who are actively searching for products and are considering buying what we sell.
Act
●	Engage and establish strong communication with the target audience.
To provide strong communication with our target audience, we will optimise the website in a way that will allow users to spend more time on the website and reduce the bounce rate. We will create a good brand story using content pillars to create content that will be shared across all social media platforms. To increase visits to physical stores, we will be verifying and listing all physical stores on Google My Business. We will launch an SEO-optimised ecommerce website to attend to the customer’s needs and allow customers to spend more time on the website. We will write SEO-optimised blog posts to target both affinity and in-market audiences. We will make the customers get familiar with the mobile app and use it for their shopping.
Convert
●	Generate sales and increase the company’s revenue.
We will be running paid ads (conversion-oriented) to the newly optimised website to generate sales and increase the company’s revenue. We will use email marketing to generate sales by sending out specialised emails geared towards generating sales. 
Engage
●	Improve customer satisfaction and build loyalty.
One of FoodCo’s missions is to provide remarkable shopping experiences to every shopper, so much so they will want to do it over again. Therefore, we will create buzz about the already existing loyalty program called “the intervention”. We will create an email campaign to notify users about specialised programs, discount, coupons and other referral programs available.




















Tactics
“Tactics without strategy is the noise before defeat.” – Sun Tzu
Website Redesign/Revamp
The most important part of our tactics is revamping and redesigning the FoodCo websites. This will allow for the smooth execution of all other tactics. We will start by revamping the FoodCo corporate website to a more user-friendly website and redesigning the main shopping website to a full ecommerce website that will run through different categories such as food, groceries and other household items. As part of the revamp, we will provide a page called “Store near me” where customers can easily search for the closest walk-in store using their location.  To attend to customer’s enquiry on the go, we will be adding a “Chatbot” to both websites. For the corporate website, the “news” section of the website will be changed to “blog”, more frequently asked questions will be added and the “about us” page will be restructured. A call-to-action button called “shop online now” will be added to three different parts of the website (header, body and footer) directing users to the redesigned ecommerce website from the corporate website.

Content Marketing
“Content builds relationships. Relationships are built on trust. Trust drives revenue.” - Andrew Davis
We will be creating a monthly content calendar using content pillars as a guide for posts to be shared on the social media pages. The content pillars will revolve around the brand, its target audience and what the brand offer. We will allocate a budget to video content as it is more engaging than text. We will be sticking with the 3 posts per week on social media.
We are going to create web copies and two SEO-optimised blog posts monthly that can rank for specific keywords related to our products and categories.
We will create at least three ad copies that communicate the brand story to our target audience per ad channel.
Finally, we will establish strong communication with customers through engaging email marketing copies.

Social Media Marketing
We will be sharing at least a post from the content calendar three times a week on each social media account to keep our target audience engaged.
We will run multiple paid ads on Facebook & Instagram (Single banner, carousel and Catalogue ads). The paid ads will be divided into four sections, targeting each user in the customer journey funnel to help achieve our objectives. 
We will run awareness, lead generation (sign up), mobile app install/ in-app registration, sales and retargeting ads on Meta Ads. This tactic will be good to target both Gen Z and Millennials.
 

Influencer Marketing  
To amplify our Top of Mind Awareness (TOMA) campaign on social media, we will work with an influencer to promote our brand. Nowadays, short-form content tends to go viral than long-form, hence we will sign a deal with an influencer to create short user-generated content (UGC) about the brand at intervals that we can co-share. These UGCs may be in form of testimonials from the influencer or offer promotions which will be measured through a referral code.

Pay Per Click (PPC)
We will be launching responsive and image Google Display ads to show on blogs, websites, mobile apps and YouTube. To capture users who are actively searching on search engines, we will launch Google search and shopping ads by targeting them through related keyword matches (broad, phrase or exact).
Occasionally, we will launch video ad campaigns on YouTube for specialised events such as black Friday. Gen Z, millennials and Gen X can be reached effectively using these tactics.
 

Email Marketing
We will be launching an email campaign divided into 4 major categories listed below
Welcome Email: This will be the first email all customers will get when they register through the ecommerce website.
Newsletters/Follow-Up Emails: We send out a series of follow-up emails for active users, non-active users, abandoned carts etc. Newsletters will go out once a week.
Sales Offers: Emails will be sent out occasionally about offers like black Friday to generate sales from fast-selling products.
Loyalty Programs/Feedback Emails: We will send out emails to notify customers about our loyalty programs. Feedback emails will also be sent out to measure customer loyalty at intervals.
 

Search Engine Optimisation
From the competitor analysis, one of the major competitors has an advantage over FoodCo when it comes to SEO. Therefore, we will be improving FoodCo’s search presence by doing the following.
On-Page SEO: The first thing we did was to carry out a website audit using an SEO audit tool to identify the website issues. After the audit, we noticed we will optimise the content on the corporate website with the necessary keywords for each page for easy ranking. We will optimise the open graph, description tags, and header and title tags for both websites. For the ecommerce website, we are going to create a good URL structure to identify website hierarchy and add image alt attributes where needed. We will be optimising the category, product pages and slugs with the right keywords.
Off-Page SEO: We will work on the Local SEO by listing and verifying all accredited physical stores on Google My Business and Bing listing. We will have guest posts about FoodCo shared on popular blogs in Nigeria. This will serve as a strong backlinks for FoodCo.  Techcabal, Nairametrics and other top news websites are good options to work with.
Technical SEO: We will improve the page loading speed for both websites, create and install Google Analytics to track all website activities, and set up a Google search console and Bing webmaster account for the ecommerce website to track all activities on both search engines. 
We will be optimising the Rank Math WordPress SEO Plugin used for the corporate website. For the ecommerce website, we will be creating a robot.txt file, a sitemap, implementing structured data markups, and more. The website audit shows that there are a lot of broken links on the foodco.ng website as a result of the last website revamp. We will be fixing all broken links by redirecting them to a new page related to the previous one using a 301 or 302 redirect.
A website audit will be carried out every two months to check for possible issues.
